# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our community is dedicated to providing a welcoming, respectful, and productive environment for all contributors.

Standards
- Be respectful and considerate in all communications.
- Assume good faith and seek to understand differing viewpoints.
- Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers.

Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers. Reports will be treated with discretion and investigated.

For the full text, see: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/
